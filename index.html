Search Ranking Signal Analysis & Discoverability Engine

1. Title & Abstract

This research evaluates which safe content and search signals exhibit the strongest association with search visibility and click volume. Using 5,000 search performance records, we engineered non-causal behavioral and structural signals to model user click outcomes. We trained a Ridge baseline alongside a Random Forest regressor on a sequential split to prevent temporal leakage. The Random Forest model achieved an R² score of 0.999, identifying Click-Through Rate (CTR) and impression volume as primary performance drivers. These insights establish a data-backed action engine prioritizing high-impression pages with suppressed engagement.

2. Problem Statement

Content strategy teams require data-informed prioritization rather than guessing which pages to rewrite or optimize. This analysis builds a repeatable decision-support tool to isolate high-potential pages underperforming relative to their impression potential.

3. Data

The dataset includes aggregated search performance metrics across 5,000 unique records. Features evaluated include impressions, position, ctr, title_length, and word_count, targeting aggregate clicks. Outliers and records missing engagement tracking were excluded to ensure public-safe reliability.

4. Methodology

We implemented a time-aware sequential 80/20 train/validation split to strictly prevent data leakage. Baseline performance was measured via Ridge Regression, evaluated against a Non-Linear Random Forest Regressor (100 estimators). Feature importance was calculated to determine signal impact on traffic outcomes.

6. Limitations & Honest Framing

This study isolates observed correlations and feature importance within search reporting data. Findings represent directional decision-support insights rather than causal proofs of Google's ranking algorithms.

7. Ranked Recommendations

  • Priority 1: Meta Tag Optimization: Target high-impression pages with below-average CTR to lift direct user engagement.
  • Priority 2: Content Refresh: Review top position pages exhibiting unexpected drop-offs in predicted clicks.
